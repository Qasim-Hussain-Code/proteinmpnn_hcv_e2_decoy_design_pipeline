# Figures for the integrated study

The [study report](../README.md) uses four figures that support its principal findings. Additional outputs provide detailed model, sequence and resource diagnostics.

## Report figures

| Figure | Purpose | Export |
|---|---|---|
| [Structural benchmark](../followup/figures/benchmark.png) | Compare directional-control recovery across receptor models and preparation modes | [PDF](../followup/figures/benchmark.pdf) |
| [Frozen held-out comparison](figure_6.png) | Show the completed strategy comparison under the failed experimental scoring gate | [PDF](figure_6.pdf) |
| [Development coverage](../followup/figures/coverage.png) | Show exact haplotype coverage within the sampled development cohorts | [PDF](../followup/figures/coverage.pdf) |
| [Coverage by recorded genotype](../followup/figures/genotype_coverage.png) | Show the conditional coverage and remaining exclusions | [PDF](../followup/figures/genotype_coverage.pdf) |

The [figure manifest](../results/figure_manifest.tsv) records titles, paths and hashes. Figures are generated from result tables with standard plotting tools; PNG supports repository viewing and PDF supports export. No figure is experimental evidence for binding by a designed computational candidate.
